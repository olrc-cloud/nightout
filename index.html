Night Out

Call three friends, find the one plan that works for everyone, then text the group to confirm it.

This weekend's options

Only one of these works for all three friends. Each friend has a different schedule, and they don't know each other's plans.

    Your steps

      Your first contact with each friend has to be a phone call. Calls are listening and speaking only: no captions. After that you can call again or send a text. The group text opens once you've called everyone.

      Transcript

      Everything you say and write is recorded. When you finish, download it and send it to your instructor.
